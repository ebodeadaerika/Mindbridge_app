**Version 1.0 | Spring 2026**

## Introduction

MindBridge is a campus wellness platform that helps university students track their mental health, connect with peers anonymously, and access support resources. This manual covers everything students and administrators need to use the platform.

**Your privacy is guaranteed.** Administrators can only see anonymised, aggregate data — they can never identify you individually.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Visit the MindBridge app URL provided by your university
2. Tap **Register** on the splash screen
3. Enter your:
   - **Full name**
   - **University email address** (must end in `.edu`, `.ac.uk`, `.ac.cm`, etc.)
   - **Password** (minimum 8 characters, at least one uppercase letter and one number)
4. Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service**
5. Tap **Create Account**
6. You are automatically logged in and taken to your Dashboard

### Signing In

1. Open MindBridge and tap **Sign In**
2. Enter your university email and password
3. Alternatively, tap **Google** to sign in with your Google account

### Signing in with Google

1. Tap **Google** on the login screen
2. Select your university Google account
3. You will be automatically logged in

### Forgot Password?

1. Tap **Forgot password?** on the login screen
2. Enter your university email address
3. Check your email for a reset link (valid for 15 minutes)
4. Click the link and enter a new password

## Student Guide

### Daily Mood Check-in

The mood check-in is the core feature of MindBridge. It takes 30 seconds and gives you — and your campus counseling team (anonymously) — a picture of student wellbeing.

**To check in:**
1. From the Dashboard, tap the mood check-in card
2. Select your **Mood Score** (1 = very low, 5 = great)
3. Select your **Energy Level** (1 = exhausted, 5 = energised)
4. Optionally add a **note** about how you're feeling
5. Tap **Submit Check-in**

**Notes:**
- You can only check in once per day
- Your check-in is completely anonymous — stored with a code, not your name
- You cannot delete a past check-in

---

### Mood History

View your mood trends over time.

1. From the Dashboard, tap **View History** or navigate to **Mood History**
2. See your daily mood and energy scores on a chart
3. Use the date picker to explore specific time periods

Your mood history is private — only you can see it.

---

### Private Journal

Write freely. Your journal entries are completely private and can never be accessed by administrators, counselors, or other students.

**Creating an entry:**
1. Navigate to **Journal** from the navigation bar
2. Tap **+** or **New Entry**
3. Enter a **title** and **body text**
4. Tap **Save**

**Editing an entry:**
1. Open the entry you want to edit
2. Tap the **edit** icon (pencil)
3. Make your changes
4. Tap **Save Changes**

**Deleting an entry:**
1. Open the entry
2. Tap **Delete**
3. Confirm deletion

> ⚠️ Deletion is permanent. There is no recycle bin.

---

### Anonymous Forum

Connect with fellow students using an automatically assigned anonymous name — such as "Silver Flamingo" or "Teal Sparrow". **No one can trace a post back to your real account**, including administrators.

**Browsing posts:**
1. Navigate to **Forum**
2. Scroll through posts or use **category filters** (Anxiety, Exams, Relationships, etc.)
3. Tap a post to read it and its replies

**Creating a post:**
1. Tap **+** or **New Post**
2. Write your message (max 500 characters)
3. Optionally select a **category**
4. Tap **Post**

Your post will appear with your randomly assigned anonymous name.

**Replying to a post:**
1. Open a post
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Type your reply and tap **Reply**

**Liking a post:**
1. Tap the ❤️ icon on any post
2. Likes are counted but cannot be traced to you

### Crisis Support

If you are struggling and need immediate support, the crisis flag allows you to confidentially alert your campus counseling team.

**Submitting a crisis flag:**
1. Navigate to **Crisis Support** from the navigation
2. Select a severity level:
   - **Low** — I'm struggling but managing
   - **Medium** — I need support soon
   - **High** — I need urgent help
3. Optionally add a message (e.g., location, preferred contact method)
4. Tap **Submit**

> ⚠️ **Important:** Your crisis flag contains NO personal information unless you choose to include it in the message. The counseling team will receive the severity level and your message — not your name or account.

**Emergency contacts (always available):**
- Emergency Services: **117**
- Mental Health Helpline: Contact your campus counseling center

### MindBot — AI Companion

MindBot is an AI-powered wellness companion available 24/7. It can listen, offer coping strategies, and guide you to professional support when needed.

**Starting a conversation:**
1. Navigate to **MindBot** from the bottom navigation
2. Type your message in the chat box
3. Tap **Send**

**What MindBot can do:**
- Listen without judgment
- Suggest breathing exercises and grounding techniques
- Help you identify and process emotions
- Guide you toward crisis support if you're in distress

**What MindBot cannot do:**
- Diagnose mental health conditions
- Replace a professional therapist or counselor
- Access your journal, mood, or other app data

> 💡 **Privacy note:** Your MindBot conversations are **never stored on the server**. They exist only in your current browser session. Closing the app clears the history.

## Admin Guide

Administrators have a different dashboard with tools for monitoring campus wellbeing and responding to alerts.

> **Admin accounts cannot access student journals, individual mood entries, or individual forum post authors.** Admin access is strictly limited to anonymised aggregate data and crisis alerts.

### Campus Dashboard

The admin dashboard shows an overview of campus mental wellness trends.

**Metrics displayed:**
- Average campus mood score (today / this week)
- Total mood check-ins submitted
- Mood distribution (how many students scored 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
- Recent crisis flags (count only — no identity)
- Active forum posts

---

### Crisis Alerts

1. Navigate to **Alerts** in the admin navigation
2. View all pending and resolved crisis flags
3. Filter by **status** (pending / resolved) or **severity** (low / medium / high)

**Resolving a crisis flag:**
1. Click on a pending flag
2. Add a **resolution note** (e.g., "Student was contacted and referred to counselor")
3. Click **Mark Resolved**

> ⚠️ Remember: crisis flags contain no student identity by default. The only information available is what the student voluntarily included in their message.

---

### Resource Management

Admins can create, edit, and delete wellness resources for students.

**Creating a resource:**
1. Navigate to **Resources** (admin view)
2. Click **+ Add Resource**
3. Fill in:
   - Title
   - Category (article / breathing / coping / hotline / meditation)
   - Description
   - URL (optional)
4. Click **Save**

**Editing a resource:**
1. Find the resource in the list
2. Click **Edit**
3. Modify and click **Save Changes**

**Deleting a resource:**
1. Find the resource
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm deletion

---

### Forum Moderation

Admins can remove posts that violate community guidelines.

1. Navigate to **Moderation** in the admin navigation
2. Browse all forum posts
3. Click on a post to view it
4. If it violates guidelines, click **Delete Post**
5. The post is permanently removed

> **Note:** Deleting a post does not reveal the poster's identity. The author's anonymity is maintained.

## Privacy & Data Policy

MindBridge is built privacy-first. Here is exactly what is and isn't stored:

| Your Action | What We Store | Can Admins See? |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Mood check-in | Anonymous code + mood score + date | Aggregate only (no names) |
| Journal entry | Your entry text (encrypted in transit) | Never |
| Forum post | Post text + anonymous name | Post text only (no real name) |
| Crisis flag | Severity + your optional message | Yes (for response) |
| Liking a post | Anonymous code | Never |
| MindBot chat | Nothing (client-only) | Never |
| Login | Email + password hash | No (hash is one-way) |

**Data retention:**
- Your account and data persist until you delete your account
- To request data deletion, contact your campus IT administrator

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can my university see which posts I write in the forum?**  
A: No. Each post is shown with an auto-generated animal name (like "Blue Sparrow"). Your real account is never linked to your posts in any response returned by the system.

**Q: Can my lecturer or counselor see my journal?**  
A: No. Journal entries are blocked at the API level — no admin token can ever retrieve them. Only you can read your journal.

**Q: Is my crisis flag anonymous?**  
A: Yes. The crisis flag table has no user identity column — it is structurally impossible for anyone to connect a crisis flag to your account, unless you voluntarily include identifying information in the message.

**Q: What happens to my MindBot conversations when I close the app?**  
A: They are deleted from your device. Nothing is stored on the server.

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**  
A: Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen. Enter your university email and follow the link sent to you.

**Q: Can I use MindBridge without a university email?**  
A: No. Registration requires a valid academic email address (.edu, .ac.uk, .ac.cm, etc.) to ensure the platform is used by enrolled students.

**Q: How many times can I check in per day?**  
A: Once per calendar day. The system prevents duplicate check-ins to ensure data integrity.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: Currently, account deletion is handled by your campus administrator. Contact your IT helpdesk to request deletion.
